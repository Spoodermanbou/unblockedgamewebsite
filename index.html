Unit 4: The Industrial Revolution

Key Terms to Remember:

  • Textile Industry: The first industry to be industrialized, starting in Great Britain.
  • Assembly Line: A manufacturing process perfected by Henry Ford for mass production.
  • Urbanization: The rapid growth of cities as people moved from rural areas to find factory jobs.
