Grand Theft Auto Complete Edition

Grand Theft Auto IV Complete Edition is an action adventure game.
This game takes place in the fictional city named Liberty City and the year is 2008.
You will play the character of Niko Bellic who is a war veteran. He is trying to have an escape from his darker past.
He is also confronting the pressure of loan sharks and the mob bosses. Niko Bellic comes to Liberty City to have his American dream.
Also he finds the man who had betrayed him in the war fifteen years earlier.

Features of Grand Theft Auto

Following are the main features of the Complete Edition:

  • Impressive action and adventure game.
  • Eleventh game in the famous GTA series.
  • Takes place in the fictional city known as Liberty City.
  • Play as war veteran Niko Bellic.
  • Game can be played in third-person perspective.
  • Lots of missions and linear scenarios included.
  • Open world game with melee attacks.
  • Multiplayer mode included with impressive graphics.
